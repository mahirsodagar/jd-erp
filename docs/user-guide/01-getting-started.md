# 1 — Getting Started

For everyone. Read this before any other chapter.

---

## 1.1 Logging in

There are **two different login pages**. Use the right one.

| You are | Go to | Page |
|---|---|---|
| A staff member (counsellor, faculty, HR, accounts, admin) | The main address your institute gave you | **Staff login** |
| A student or a parent | The same address, but with `/#/portal/login` at the end | **Portal login** |

On either page:

1. Type your **username or your email address** — both work in the same box.
2. Type your password.
3. Select **Log in**.

If you are a student or parent, the system takes you straight to your portal
after login. Staff land on the Dashboard.

### First-time login

You will have been sent a username and a temporary password — students and
parents by email, staff usually by your HR or admin team. Log in with it, then
change your password straight away (see §1.5).

### Failed login attempts

After **5 wrong passwords** your account is locked for **15 minutes**. Waiting
is the only fix; the lock clears by itself. If you are certain the password is
right, ask your administrator to reset it rather than guessing again.

## 1.2 Your session

You are signed out automatically after about **45 minutes of inactivity**.
While you are working, the system keeps you signed in silently — you should
never be interrupted mid-task.

If you do get signed out, the login page shows a "your session expired"
message. Log in again; nothing you had already saved is lost. **Anything you
had typed but not saved is lost**, so save long forms as you go.

## 1.3 Finding your way around

The staff screen has three parts:

- **The left sidebar** — the menu, grouped by area of work. Select a group to
  expand it.
- **The top bar** — your name, and the way out (log out).
- **The main area** — whatever you are working on.

On a narrow screen or a phone, the sidebar is hidden behind a menu button.

### The menu groups

| Group | What's inside |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your home screen, plus your Daily Report and Admin Daily Report |
| **Leads** | All Leads, Add Lead, Entrance Exam, Lead Reports, Counsellor Pools |
| **Admission** | Students, Fee Collection, Batch Promotion, Fee Reports, Concession Reports, Fee Templates |
| **LMS** | Assignments, Courseware, Lessons, Marks, Tests |
| **Slot** | New Slot, Publish Timetable, Calendar, Timetable, Time Slots |
| **Academics** | Attendance, Batch Report, Closing Report, Records (certificates, alumni, transcripts), 0-Hour Form, Student Leaves, Document Requests, Appointments |
| **HR** | Employees, New Employee, Departments, Designations, Relieving |
| **Leaves** | My Leaves, Apply Leave, Comp-Off, Team Approvals, Balances, Allocations, Leave Report, Leave Types |
| **Tasks** | My Tasks, Tasks Report |
| **Forms to fill** | Forms addressed to your role, and the ones you have already submitted |
| **Audit** | Dashboards and every internal report type |
| **Master Data** | The reference lists — campuses, programs, batches, subjects and so on |
| **Admin** | Users, New User, Roles & Permissions |
| **Settings** | Your own preferences |

Some entries appear only for students — "My Assignments", "My Tests",
"My Timetable", "My Attendance", "My Transcript", "My Certificates".

## 1.4 Why can't I see a menu item?

This is the most common question, and it almost always has one of three
answers.

**1. You haven't been given that permission.**
Menu items you cannot use are hidden entirely. Ask your administrator to add
the permission to your role.

**2. You were given the permission but haven't logged out and back in.**
Your access is loaded when you log in. After an administrator changes your
role, **log out and log back in** — otherwise you will keep seeing the old
menu.

**3. It's a self-service page that is simply empty for you.**
A few pages are visible to everyone but only show work addressed to *you*:

| Page | Shows |
|---|---|
| Team Approvals (Leaves) | Leave requests from people who report to you |
| Student Leaves (Academics) | Leave requests from batches you mentor |
| Appointments (Academics) | Meeting requests addressed to you |
| Relieving (HR) | Your own exit application, and ones you must approve |
| My Tasks | Tasks you created or were assigned |
| Forms to fill | Forms published to a role you hold |

If you don't mentor a batch, the Student Leaves page will be empty. That is
correct behaviour, not a fault.

## 1.5 Changing your password

Go to **Settings** and use the change-password option. You will need your
current password.

Password rules:

- At least **8 characters**
- Not entirely numbers
- Not a well-known common password
- Not too similar to your name, username or email

Students and parents change their password from **My Profile** in the portal.

## 1.6 Forgotten password

On the login page, select **Forgot password**. Enter your email address and the
system emails you a reset link.

Notes:

- The link is single-use and expires. If it stops working, request a new one.
- You can only request a handful of resets per hour. This is a safety limit.
- If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder first, then ask your
  administrator — they can reset your password directly.

## 1.7 About campuses

Your account is assigned to one or more campuses. Nearly every list — students,
employees, leads, fees, attendance reports — shows only records from **your**
campuses.

Some senior roles hold a "see all campuses" permission and see everything. If
you need cross-campus visibility for your job, that is a request to your
administrator, not something you can switch on yourself.
