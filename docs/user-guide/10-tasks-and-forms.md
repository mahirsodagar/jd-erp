# 10 — Tasks & Forms

For everyone. Menu groups: **Tasks** and **Forms to fill**.

Both are self-service — everyone has them, and neither needs a permission to
use.

---

## 10.1 Tasks

**Tasks → My Tasks.**

A simple to-do list you can assign to colleagues, or to yourself.

### Creating a task

Give it a **name**, a **description**, an **end date** (when you expect it
finished) and an **assignee**.

> One rule: **you cannot assign two tasks with the same name to the same
> person.** If you get an error saying the task already exists, either the task
> is already there or you need a more specific name.

### Working a task

The assignee can:

- add **remarks** as they go — a running note of progress;
- mark the task **Completed** when it's done, which records the completion
  time.

Both the person who created the task and the person assigned it can see it.

### The Tasks Report

**Tasks → Tasks Report** shows every user's tasks across the institute. This is
the one part of Tasks that needs a permission — it is for team leads and
management.

Deleting someone else's task also needs a permission.

## 10.2 Forms to fill

**Forms to fill → Forms to fill.**

The institute publishes internal forms — feedback, checklists, declarations,
surveys — targeted at particular roles. If a form is published to a role you
hold, it appears here.

**No permission is needed.** If the list is empty, no forms are currently
addressed to your role.

### Filling one

Open the form and complete the fields. Forms can contain:

- short text and long text boxes
- single-choice options (radio buttons or a dropdown)
- multiple-choice options (checkboxes or a multi-select list)
- star ratings
- dates, times, and date-and-time fields

Required fields are marked. Submit when you're done.

**You can submit the same form more than once** if the institute asks you to —
for example a monthly checklist.

### What you've already sent

**Forms to fill → My Submissions** lists everything you have submitted, so you
can check what you said and when.

You cannot edit a submission after sending it. If you made a mistake, submit a
corrected response and tell whoever collects the form.

## 10.3 Creating forms

That side of the feature — designing forms, choosing which roles see them, and
reading everyone's responses — lives under **Audit** and is covered in
[chapter 11](11-audit-reports.md) §11.7.

## 10.4 What your actions affect

| When you… | This happens elsewhere |
|---|---|
| Assign a task | The assignee sees it in My Tasks and is emailed |
| Complete a task | The creator sees it as done, with the time recorded |
| Submit a form | It appears in the audit team's Filled Forms list, with your name |
