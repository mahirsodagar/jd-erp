# 12 — Student & Parent Portal

For students and parents. This is the only chapter you need.

---

## 12.1 Logging in

Go to the address your institute gave you, ending in **`/#/portal/login`**.

Use the **username and password** that were emailed to you when your account
was created. You can type either your username or your email address in the
first box.

**Didn't get the email?** Check your spam folder. If it isn't there, contact
the admissions office — they can send it again.

**Forgotten your password?** Select **Forgot password** on the login page and
enter your email address. You'll get a reset link. If that doesn't work, the
admissions office can reset it for you.

**Locked out?** After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. Wait,
then try again.

## 12.2 Changing your password

Go to **My Profile** and change your password there.

Your new password must be at least 8 characters, not all numbers, not a common
password, and not too similar to your name or email.

Do this the first time you log in.

## 12.4 Attendance

**Attendance** shows two views: a calendar of your sessions, and a report with
your percentage.

Your status for each session is one of:

| Status | Counts as present? |
|---|---|
| Present | Yes |
| Late | Yes |
| On Duty | Yes — official institute business |
| Excused | No |
| Absent | No |

### Why your percentage might look low

Your percentage is worked out against **every class that was scheduled**, not
just the ones where the register was taken. If a faculty member has not yet
marked a class, it counts against you until they do.

If your percentage looks wrong, speak to your batch mentor before assuming
there is a mistake in your record — they can check whether registers are
outstanding.

## 12.5 Timetable

**Timetable** shows your classes for the week: subject, faculty, classroom and
time.

If a class is cancelled it disappears from your timetable. Check it each
morning.

## 12.6 Assignments

**Assignments** lists work set for your batch, with the due date and marks
available.

To submit: open the assignment and upload a file, type your response, or both —
whatever the assignment asks for.

Things to know:

- **You can resubmit.** A new submission replaces the previous one. The latest
  version is what gets marked.
- **Late submissions are accepted but marked as late.**
- **Your faculty can give you a personal extension**, which changes your
  deadline without changing anyone else's.
- Once marked, you'll see your **grade and feedback** on the assignment.
- If your faculty asks you to **resubmit**, read their feedback, revise, and
  submit again.

## 12.7 Examination

**Examination** lists online tests set for you.

Before you start, check:

- the **window** — the earliest you can start and the latest you can submit;
- the **duration** — how long you get **once you start**.

> Your time starts when you start the test. Make sure you have a stable
> connection and enough uninterrupted time before you begin.

Multiple-choice answers are marked immediately. Short-answer questions are read
and marked by your faculty, so your final result may take a little longer.

View your result under the test once it's available.

## 12.8 Course Ware

**Course Ware** holds notes, slides and reference material published by your
faculty, organised by subject and topic. Download whatever you need.

> If material your classmates can see is missing for you, it's usually because
> you joined the batch after that topic was published. Ask your faculty to
> republish it.

## 12.9 Lessons

**Lessons** shows your faculty's lesson plans — what will be covered, the work
set and when it's due.

A plan only appears once both reviewers have approved it, and sometimes only
from a date the faculty chose. So a plan may exist without being visible to you
yet.

## 12.10 Applying for leave

**Apply Leaves.** Give the start date, the end date and your reason.

Your request goes to your **batch mentor**. You'll see the outcome here and
receive an email.

Apply in advance where you can. If you've been unwell and are applying
afterwards, say so in the reason.

## 12.11 Requesting a document

**Apply Documents.** Request an official letter or certificate from the office:

- Transfer Certificate
- Bonafide Certificate
- Study Certificate
- Bank Letter
- Letter of Recommendation
- Something else — describe what you need

**Explain what it's for.** The purpose you give determines what the office
writes, so be specific: "for a student bank account", "for a visa
application", "for an internship at X".

Once approved, you can download the document from **My Documents**. If it's
rejected you'll see the reason.

## 12.12 Booking an appointment

**Appointments.** Request a meeting with either an office team — Management,
Admissions, Accounts, Academics, Examination or Placement — or with a named
faculty member.

Give your preferred date and time and the reason.

What happens next:

- They **confirm** — possibly at a different time, and usually with a venue.
  Check the confirmed details, not the ones you asked for.
- Or they **decline**, with a reason.
- You can **cancel** your request while it's still open.

Two things to know:

- **You can only have one open request with the same team or person at a
  time.** Wait for a reply before asking again.
- **You'll only be told inside the portal** — no SMS, WhatsApp or email is sent
  for appointments. Check back here.

## 12.13 My Documents

Your certificates and documents in one place: what you uploaded with your
application, and anything the office has issued to you.

## 12.14 Feedback Links

Where you give feedback on your course, when your institute opens it.

**Your feedback is anonymous to your faculty.** They see only combined ratings
across the class, never who said what. Please be honest — it's how teaching
gets better.

## 12.15 Fees

Your fee position — what's due, what you've paid, and printable receipts — is
available in the portal. You can download a copy of any receipt.

If a payment you've made isn't showing, allow a day for the accounts office to
record it, then contact them with your transaction reference.

## 12.16 For parents

If your child's institute has created a parent account for you, you log in the
same way, at the same portal address, with your own username and password.

**What you can see:** the dashboard, attendance, timetable, lesson plans and
your child's profile.

**What you cannot do:** submit assignments, take tests, apply for leave, or
book appointments. Those are your child's to do — the account is deliberately
read-only.

If you don't have a parent account and would like one, ask the admissions
office.

## 12.17 Common questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I can't log in | Check you're on the **portal** login page, not the staff one. Then try Forgot password |
| My attendance looks wrong | Some registers may not be marked yet. Speak to your batch mentor |
| An assignment is missing | It may not be published yet, or it may be set for a different batch |
| Course material is missing | You may have joined the batch after it was published — ask your faculty to republish |
| My marks aren't showing | Marks appear only after your HOD publishes them |
| My test result isn't complete | Short-answer questions are marked by hand and take longer |
| Nobody replied to my appointment | Check the portal — appointment updates only appear here |
| I've paid but my balance is unchanged | Allow a day, then contact the accounts office with your transaction reference |
